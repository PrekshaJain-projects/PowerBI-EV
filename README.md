🚗 Electric Vehicle Market Analytics Dashboard
📊 Overview:
This project showcases an interactive Power BI Dashboard designed to visualize Electric Vehicle (EV) market trends, revenue performance, and manufacturer insights.
The dataset was synthetically generated using Python, simulating realistic EV sales and service data to replicate real-world business scenarios.
The dashboard helps decision-makers analyze key performance indicators (KPIs), understand customer trends, and identify high-performing manufacturers — all within a visually intuitive interface.

🎯 Objectives
-Examine EV market growth trends over time.
-Measure total revenue, growth rate, and units sold.
-Compare manufacturer and model-level performance.
-Evaluate service cost distribution by cost category.
-Enable interactive insights using vehicle type and year filters.

⚙️ Tools & Techniques
🐍 Python
Used to generate synthetic EV datasets using libraries like Pandas, NumPy, and Faker.
Ensured realistic distributions for sales, revenue, service cost, and customer data.
Exported a clean, structured dataset to Excel for Power BI integration.

📊 Microsoft Excel
Used for data cleaning, column renaming, and initial validation.
Verified consistency of generated data before importing into Power BI.

💡 Power BI
Built an interactive dashboard using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) to calculate metrics such as:
-Revenue Growth %
-Total Revenue
-Total Units Sold
-Invoice Count
-Average Revenue per Customer
Created relationships between data tables for drill-down analysis.
Designed dynamic visuals including KPIs, matrix tables, ribbon charts, and pie charts for a visually rich experience.

📈 Dashboard Features
🔹 KPIs
Total Revenue – Measures overall revenue from EV sales.
Revenue Growth % – Tracks year-over-year growth trend.
Total Units Sold – Shows cumulative units sold across all models.
Invoice Count – Displays total number of sales invoices recorded.

🔹 Visuals
Matrix Table: Shows manufacturer, model, and revenue contribution hierarchy.
Ribbon Chart: Displays changes in revenue per customer by manufacturer over time.
Pie Chart: Represents service distribution by cost category.
Filters: Enable dynamic insights by vehicle type and year.

🔍 Insights
Steady Growth in EV Revenue: The dashboard shows a consistent increase in total revenue, indicating rising market adoption.
Top Performing Manufacturers: Certain manufacturers dominate total revenue and customer retention, showcasing strong market leadership.
Customer-Level Trends: The ribbon chart highlights shifts in customer loyalty and spending patterns between different manufacturers.
Balanced Cost Distribution: The service cost pie chart reveals a healthy balance among various cost categories, helping assess operational efficiency.
Yearly Comparison: Interactive filters help users analyze how revenue growth and sales volume evolved across multiple years and vehicle types.
